My journey into advertising started 12 years ago. For most of my career, I've spent it working agency side in industry titans the likes of Ogilvy, Leo Burnett & other network giants. While the rest of my time has dedicated to advertising-adjacent work (be it production, events, start-ups).

12 years of experience and still hungry for more.

Knowing why is just as
important as knowing how.

I am a firm believer that creative work leans strongly on sharp strategy — a truth that should never be compromised on. In 2015, I pivoted into Strategy to thoroughly understand the craft & methodology — molding me into the pragmatic creative I am today.

Meaningful work isn't all just an Aha moment
— it takes work & a learner mindset.

Visuals speak
a thousand words.

As an Art Director / Illustrator, my work has covered a broad range of artistic endeavours including branding, visual identity, print, TVCs, digital content, packaging design, merchandise design, guerrilla campaigns, activation, etc. Outside of advertising, I've had the privilege to work on music videos, music festivals and restaurants.

I believe design should be clever, functional & strategically imbued — a principle I strive to incorporate into all my work.
